# Security Policy

## Scope

This policy covers potential vulnerabilities in VahanaSec's code, tests, documentation examples, and project infrastructure maintained by the project.

Check the repository's current status before assuming a component is supported or actively maintained.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please do not disclose sensitive vulnerability details in public issues, pull requests, discussions, or social media posts before responsible coordination.

If GitHub private vulnerability reporting is enabled for this repository, use it to submit the report.

If it is unavailable, contact a maintainer through a verified private channel published by ARC. Do not send sensitive details to unrelated contributors.

## Include in Your Report

Where possible, include:

- Affected component and version or commit.
- Description and potential impact.
- Reproduction steps using a simulated or authorised environment.
- Minimal proof-of-concept material.
- Relevant logs with secrets and personal information removed.
- Suggested remediation, if known.

Do not include live credentials, private keys, proprietary firmware, or confidential vehicle data.

## Research Boundaries

This policy does not authorise testing third-party vehicles, OEM infrastructure, public services, or external systems.

Use owned, simulated, or explicitly authorised environments. Avoid actions that could cause physical harm, damage, privacy violations, or service disruption.

For vulnerabilities affecting third-party products, contact the relevant owner through its authorised reporting process.

## Handling and Disclosure

Maintainers may assess the report, request clarification, investigate affected versions, and coordinate remediation and disclosure with the reporter.

Response times and remediation are not guaranteed. The project may not have dedicated security personnel.

Please allow reasonable time for coordination before publishing sensitive details.

## Limitations

This policy does not provide blanket legal safe harbour or grant permission to test systems outside the authorised scope.

## Recognition

With the reporter's consent, meaningful security contributions may be acknowledged in project documentation or release notes. Recognition and financial rewards are not guaranteed.

## Reporting Channel

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature when available. Otherwise, use a verified private maintainer contact published by ARC.

Do not post sensitive vulnerability details publicly while searching for a reporting contact.
